## Good afternoon

It is almost three twenty in the afternoon eastern time as I type this. 
Hope you are doing well. 

I have an interesting terminal based project for you. 
It is called TermRacer and it is built almost exclusively by Anthorpic Claude. 
It is available at 
https://github.com/kusl/TermRacer 
and the readme describes it as 
> Top-down racing in the terminal. One circuit, a timed lap or endless laps, and an autopilot you can hand the car to at any moment.
Its requirements are: 
- .NET 10 SDK (any 10.0 feature band; see global.json)
- A 24-bit colour terminal of at least 120 × 40 cells. Smaller windows get a resize prompt and the game waits.
It has two dependencies Terminal.Gui, and xunit.v3. 
Take a look and star it if you like it!
