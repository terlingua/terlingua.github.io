## Where to read

Technical chapter E, book pages 291–293 (PDF pages 304–306 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Define degree-days and temperature demand
- Use temperature demand to quantify thermostat savings
- State the three ways to reduce building energy consumption
