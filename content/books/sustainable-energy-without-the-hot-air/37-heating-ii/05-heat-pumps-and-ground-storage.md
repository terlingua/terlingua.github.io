## Where to read

Technical chapter E, book pages 300–306 (PDF pages 313–319 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the ideal efficiency of a heat pump
- Size a rock heat store and explain how the ground insulates it
- Estimate the limit on ground-source heat pumps in a suburb
- Explain when thermal mass helps and when it hurts
