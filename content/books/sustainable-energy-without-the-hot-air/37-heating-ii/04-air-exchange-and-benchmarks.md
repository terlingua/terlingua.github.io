## Where to read

Technical chapter E, book pages 296–300 (PDF pages 309–313 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain how a heat exchanger works like a nose
- Describe the Heatkeeper house's performance (with errata)
- Compare offices and houses using power per unit floor area

## Errata that affect this section

- Pages 298–299 — the top line of page 298 gives 6.6 W/m² as the Heatkeeper house's total power per unit area; this is the heating power only. The total is 12.2 W/m². The error is repeated in Figure E.12 (p299). MacKay's house "after" uses 6.2 W/m² of gas and 7.1 W/m² in total.
- Page 299 — PassivHaus standards define consumption in terms of "primary energy", so they are actually more stringent than Figure E.12 shows; how much more depends on the fuel mix.
