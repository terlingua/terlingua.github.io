## Where to read

Chapter 19, book pages 114–115 (PDF pages 127–128 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why "if everyone does X" statements mislead
- List three ways to reduce demand and three ways to increase supply
