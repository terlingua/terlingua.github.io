## Where to read

Chapter 29, book pages 223–228 (PDF pages 236–241 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why markets alone won't deliver long-term energy decisions
- Explain "greening the tax system" and the case for a carbon tax
- Describe the admission barrier, small cost differences, and Larry and Tina
