## Where to read

Chapter 29, book pages 222–223 (PDF pages 235–236 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the "single crude brush-stroke" solution to climate change
- Describe ways to guarantee a high carbon price
