## Where to read

Chapter 30, book pages 235–239 (PDF pages 248–252 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate world wind, hydro, tide, wave, geothermal and energy crops per person
- State the bottom line for the world

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 238, paragraph 2 — the Brazilian sugarcane power density is affected by the page 284 erratum (corrected figure about 0.5 W/m², with Andrew Ferguson estimating about 0.29 W/m²).
