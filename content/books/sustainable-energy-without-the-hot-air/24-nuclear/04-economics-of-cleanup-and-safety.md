## Where to read

Chapter 24, book pages 167–169 (PDF pages 180–182 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate the clean-up cost per kWh and compare it with wind subsidy
- Describe the THORP leak and its lessons
- Use "deaths per GWy" to compare electricity sources

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 167 — after discussing clean-up costs, add "Moreover, most of this nuclear clean-up cost is associated with weapons-making facilities, not civilian power stations."
