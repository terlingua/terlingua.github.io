## Where to read

Chapter 24, book pages 162–166 (PDF pages 175–179 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate 1000-year uranium power for once-through and fast breeder reactors
- Compare mined, ocean and river uranium
- Discuss the cost and scale of extracting uranium from seawater
