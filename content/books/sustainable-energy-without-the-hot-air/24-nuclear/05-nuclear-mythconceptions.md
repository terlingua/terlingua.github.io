## Where to read

Chapter 24, book pages 169–171 (PDF pages 182–184 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Calculate construction's contribution to nuclear carbon intensity
- Compare nuclear waste volumes with other wastes
- Expose the "magic playing field" in build-rate arguments

## Errata that affect this section

- Pages 169–170 — "after 1000 years, the radioactivity of the high-level waste is about the same as that of uranium ore" should read "if we reprocess the waste, separating off the uranium and plutonium for reuse in new nuclear fuel, then after 1000 years, the radioactivity of the high-level waste is about the same as that of uranium ore."
