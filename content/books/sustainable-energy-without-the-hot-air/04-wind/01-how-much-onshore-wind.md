## Where to read

Chapter 4, book pages 32–33 (PDF pages 45–46 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Compute wind power per person from power per unit area and area per person
- State the two "facts worth remembering" from Chapter 4
- Explain load factor and use it to check the 2 W/m² figure
