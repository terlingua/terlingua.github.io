## Where to read

Chapter 26, book pages 187–189 (PDF pages 200–202 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Quantify the fastest slew in Irish wind data and scale it to Britain
- Compare wind slew with daily demand slew
- Quantify the energy needed to ride through a five-day lull
