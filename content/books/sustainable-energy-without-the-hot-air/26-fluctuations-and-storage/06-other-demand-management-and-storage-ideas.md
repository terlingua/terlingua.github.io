## Where to read

Chapter 26, book pages 196–200 (PDF pages 209–213 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

- Describe frequency-sensitive fridges, Denmark's solution and Fair Isle's two grids
- Compare storage technologies by energy density, efficiency and lifetime
