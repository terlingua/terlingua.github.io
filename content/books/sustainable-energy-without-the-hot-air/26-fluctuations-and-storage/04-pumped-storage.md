## Where to read

Chapter 26, book pages 190–194 (PDF pages 203–207 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Describe Britain's existing pumped storage and Dinorwig's statistics
- Estimate the lakes needed to store 1200 GWh
- Assess how far pumped storage could be scaled up

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 192, Table 26.7 — in columns 2 and 3, all volumes (40, 40, 100…) and depths (20, 10, 20…) should be doubled, to 80, 80, 200… and 40, 20, 40… respectively.
