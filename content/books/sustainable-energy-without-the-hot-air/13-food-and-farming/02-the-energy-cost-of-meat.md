## Where to read

Chapter 13, book pages 77–78 (PDF pages 90–91 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why meat requires feeding a "queue" of animals
- Derive the 8 kWh/d meat estimate and 12 kWh/d diet estimate
- Explain when grazing animals may be the best use of land
