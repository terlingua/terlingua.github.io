## Where to read

Technical chapter A, book pages 261–262 (PDF pages 274–275 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Relate battery energy density, battery mass and range
- Explain why modern batteries solve the range problem
