## Where to read

Technical chapter A, book pages 258–261 (PDF pages 271–274 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain rolling resistance and the coefficient Crr
- Find the speed where rolling resistance equals air resistance, using the errata
- Explain why real cars don't follow v² exactly

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 260 — the speed at which a car's rolling resistance equals air resistance is wrong: "7 m/s = 16 miles per hour" should be replaced by "13 m/s = 29 miles per hour".
