## Where to read

Preface and Chapter 1, book pages 5–13 (PDF pages 18–26 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the three steps of the climate-change argument
- Explain why large natural CO2 flows do not make human emissions insignificant
- Read logarithmic graphs and explain why they show growth rates
- Compare per-capita emissions of regions and countries
