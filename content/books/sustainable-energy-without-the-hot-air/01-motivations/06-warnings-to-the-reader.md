## Where to read

Preface and Chapter 1, book pages 16–18 (PDF pages 29–31 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why the book deliberately rounds numbers
- Distinguish factual assertions from ethical assertions
- Explain why the book focuses on factors of 2 and 10 rather than economics
