## Where to read

Preface and Chapter 1, book pages 14–16 (PDF pages 27–29 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why cumulative emissions matter more than current rates
- Describe the size of cuts needed for a reasonable chance of avoiding 2 °C
- State the share of world emissions due to energy use
