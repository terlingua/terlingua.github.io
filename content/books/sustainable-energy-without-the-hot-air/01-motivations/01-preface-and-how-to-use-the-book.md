## Where to read

Preface, book pages viii–ix (PDF pages 9–10 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the problem the author says he wants to cut emissions of
- Describe the two senses in which the book is free
- Explain how chapters, technical chapters, endnotes and TinyURL codes are organised
