## Where to read

Preface and Chapter 1, book pages 2–4 (PDF pages 15–17 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why "huge" is not a useful description of an energy source
- Recognise misleading uses of big numbers and switched comparisons
- List the kinds of question the book's numbers are meant to answer

## Errata that affect this section

- Pages 2 and 18 — Porritt's first name is spelled "Jonathon", not "Jonathan".
