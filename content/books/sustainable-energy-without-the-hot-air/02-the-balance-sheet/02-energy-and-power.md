## Where to read

Chapter 2, book pages 24–26 (PDF pages 37–39 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish energy from power using the water-tap analogy
- Convert between W, kW and kWh/d
- Explain why "kilowatt per second" is wrong
- Explain why the book uses per-person units
