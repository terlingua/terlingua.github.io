## Where to read

Chapter 10, book pages 60–61 (PDF pages 73–74 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the power per unit area assumed for offshore wind
- Estimate the shallow offshore resource and the area it needs
- Describe the engineering difficulties of turbines at sea
