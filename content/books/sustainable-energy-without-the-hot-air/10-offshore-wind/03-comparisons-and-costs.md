## Where to read

Chapter 10, book pages 62–63 (PDF pages 75–76 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Contrast the ease of consuming with the difficulty of producing
- Compare the steel and concrete in offshore wind with other big projects
- Estimate the cost of 33 GW of offshore wind and compare with microturbines

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 62, line 14 from the bottom — "0.14 million tons" should read "140 million tons".
