## Where to read

Chapter 10, book pages 63–64 (PDF pages 76–77 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Put bird deaths from wind turbines into numerical context

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 63 — "Denmark, where windmills generate 9% of the electricity" should read "19%". (Danish wind production in 2008 was 3.4 kWh/d/p; total gross electricity production was 18 kWh/d/p.)
