## Where to read

Chapter 18, book pages 104–106 (PDF pages 117–119 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Compare the red stack with official American, European and UK averages
- Identify flows missing from official "primary energy" figures
- Explain the link between GDP and energy consumption, and its exception
