## Where to read

Chapter 3, book pages 30–31 (PDF pages 43–44 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain what a "boundary" is in an energy estimate
- Explain why a driver's consumption differs from the national average
- Distinguish gross and net calorific values

## Errata that affect this section

- Pages 30–31, note 29 — in the sentence about "the average use of energy for car driving", both "24"s should be "13". 24 kWh/d/p was the average for all road transport; 13 kWh/d/p goes into cars and motorcycles.
