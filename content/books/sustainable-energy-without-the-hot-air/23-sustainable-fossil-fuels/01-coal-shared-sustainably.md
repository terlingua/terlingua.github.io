## Where to read

Chapter 23, book pages 157–158 (PDF pages 170–171 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Apply the 1000-year definition of sustainable use to world and UK coal
- Explain the energy penalty of carbon capture and storage
- Explain why clean coal is only a stop-gap
