## Where to read

Technical chapter C, book pages 274–276 (PDF pages 287–289 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Derive the gross transport cost of any plane (about 0.4 kWh/ton-km)
- Compare passenger efficiency with cars
- Explain why speed limits for planes make no sense
