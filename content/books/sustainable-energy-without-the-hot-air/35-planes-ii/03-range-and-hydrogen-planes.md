## Where to read

Technical chapter C, book pages 276–278 (PDF pages 289–291 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Derive the range of any bird or plane from the fuel's intrinsic distance
- Explain why hydrogen planes won't be radically more efficient
- Identify formation flying and hop-length optimization as improvements
