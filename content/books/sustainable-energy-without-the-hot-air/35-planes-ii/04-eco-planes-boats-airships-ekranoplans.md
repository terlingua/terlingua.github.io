## Where to read

Technical chapter C, book pages 278–282 (PDF pages 291–295 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Test the Electra electric plane against the 0.4 kWh/ton-km benchmark
- Apply the flight theory to hydrofoils
- Derive the transport cost of an airship and an ekranoplan
