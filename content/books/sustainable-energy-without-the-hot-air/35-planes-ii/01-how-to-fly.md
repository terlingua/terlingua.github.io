## Where to read

Technical chapter C, book pages 269–273 (PDF pages 282–286 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain how planes stay up by throwing air down
- Show that at the optimal speed lift power equals drag power
- Predict the optimal speeds of a 747 and an albatross
