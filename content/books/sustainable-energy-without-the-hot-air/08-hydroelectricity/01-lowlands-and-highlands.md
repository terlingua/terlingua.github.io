## Where to read

Chapter 8, book pages 55–56 (PDF pages 68–69 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Calculate the gravitational power per unit area of rainfall
- Explain why lowland hydro can never exceed about 1 kWh/d per person
- Derive the plausible practical hydro estimate of 1.5 kWh/d per person

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 55, map — Kinlochewe should be located about 60 km further north.
