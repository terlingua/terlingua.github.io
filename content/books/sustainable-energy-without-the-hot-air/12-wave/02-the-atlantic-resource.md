## Where to read

Chapter 12, book pages 74–75 (PDF pages 87–88 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate the UK's raw Atlantic wave power per person
- Derive the plausible 4 kWh/d estimate
- Compare the Pelamis's performance and steel with offshore wind

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 75, last line — "5%" should read "10%" (the Limpet's actual output as a share of its prediction).
