## Where to read

Technical chapter H, book pages 322–324 (PDF pages 335–337 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Compare estimates of the carbon embodied in Britain's imports
- Estimate the embodied energy of imports from their weight

## Errata that affect this section
