## Where to read

Technical chapter D, book pages 283–284 (PDF pages 296–297 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate power per unit area for energy crops in power stations
- Compare liquid-fuel crops: rape, sugar beet, sugar cane, corn, switchgrass, jatropha

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 284, sugar cane — "17 600 l of ethanol" … "1.2 W/m²" should read "6500 l of ethanol" … "0.5 W/m²". According to Andrew Ferguson, the power density of Brazilian sugarcane ethanol is about 0.29 W/m².
- Page 284, corn in the USA — "0.02 W/m²" should read "0.2 W/m²". Processing uses about 83 000 of the 84 000 BTU per gallon, so 99% of the energy is used up and the net is about 0.002 W/m²; counting all co-products, about 0.05 W/m².
