## Where to read

Technical chapter D, book pages 286–288 (PDF pages 299–301 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Estimate the power per unit area of food crops
- Estimate electricity from straw, landfill methane and burning household waste

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 286, paragraph 2, line 4 — "If 2800 m² of Britain…" should read "If 2800 m² per person of Britain…".
