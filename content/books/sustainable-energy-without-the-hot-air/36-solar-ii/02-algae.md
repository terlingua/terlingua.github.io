## Where to read

Technical chapter D, book pages 284–286 (PDF pages 297–299 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why CO2-fed algae ponds achieve higher power density
- Assess the CO2 and land limits of algae biodiesel
- Assess algae at sea and hydrogen-producing algae

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 285 — end of paragraph 1: "230 square metres … roughly 6%" should read "100 square metres … roughly 3%".
