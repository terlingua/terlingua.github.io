## Where to read

Technical chapter B, book pages 263–264 (PDF pages 276–277 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Derive the power of the wind through a hoop
- State the Betz limit
- Estimate the output of a single windmill

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 263, Figure B.1 — for Beaufort force 7, "31 km/h" should be replaced by "58 km/h".
