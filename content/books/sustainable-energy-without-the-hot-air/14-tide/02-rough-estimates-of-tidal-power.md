## Where to read

Chapter 14, book pages 82–84 (PDF pages 95–97 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the power density of tide-pools for various ranges
- Explain why the North Sea is a natural tide-pool
- Estimate the raw incoming tidal power and a first guess at extractable power
