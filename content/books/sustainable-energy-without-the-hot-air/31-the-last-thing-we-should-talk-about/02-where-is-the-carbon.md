## Where to read

Chapter 31, book pages 241–244 (PDF pages 254–257 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- State the sizes of the main carbon pools
- Explain why the extra carbon will affect the climate for 1000 years
- Describe positive feedbacks such as ocean acidification and methane hydrates

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 241, Figure 31.2 — the 600 Gt of carbon shown in the atmosphere is the pre-industrial amount; since 1850 it has increased to roughly 800 Gt.
