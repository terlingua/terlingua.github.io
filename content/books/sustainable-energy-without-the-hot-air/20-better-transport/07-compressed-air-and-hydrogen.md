## Where to read

Chapter 20, book pages 129–131 (PDF pages 142–144 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain the limitations of compressed-air energy storage
- Explain why hydrogen is an energy carrier, not a source, and its inefficiencies

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 131 — the claim that hydrogen "gradually leaks out of any practical container" (with the one-week parking example) is incorrect. It should read that hydrogen gradually boils off from cryogenic tanks to keep them cold; park a cryogenic hydrogen car with a full tank and come back two or three weeks later, and most of the hydrogen has gone.
