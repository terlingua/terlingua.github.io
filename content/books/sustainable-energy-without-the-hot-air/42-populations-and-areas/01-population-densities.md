## Where to read

Data chapter J, book pages 338–341 (PDF pages 351–354 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Read population density from a log–log plot of area versus population
- Identify area-rich countries with low population density
