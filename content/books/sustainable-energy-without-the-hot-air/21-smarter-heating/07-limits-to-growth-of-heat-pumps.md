## Where to read

Chapter 21, book pages 152–154 (PDF pages 165–167 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain why the ground is not a limitless heat source
- State the sustainable ground-extraction limit in a dense suburb
- State the chapter's conclusion about heating
