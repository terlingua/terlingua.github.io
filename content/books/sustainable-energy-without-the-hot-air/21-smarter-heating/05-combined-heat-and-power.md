## Where to read

Chapter 21, book pages 144–150 (PDF pages 157–163 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain how CHP works and why its heat is not "free"
- Explain why "total efficiency" is a misleading measure
- Critique the idea that decentralization is automatically greener
