## Where to read

Chapter 21, book pages 146–151 (PDF pages 159–164 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Explain how a heat pump works and define coefficient of performance
- Distinguish air-source and ground-source heat pumps
- Explain why heat pumps beat condensing boilers even on gas-fired electricity
