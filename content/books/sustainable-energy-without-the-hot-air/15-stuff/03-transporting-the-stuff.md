## Where to read

Chapter 15, book pages 90–93 (PDF pages 103–106 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Use ton-kilometres to measure freight
- Compare freight energy intensities by road and ship
- Estimate the energy of water delivery, sewage and desalination
