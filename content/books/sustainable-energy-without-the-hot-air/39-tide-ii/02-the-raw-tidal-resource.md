## Where to read

Technical chapter G, book pages 311–315 (PDF pages 324–328 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- Use the shallow-water wave formula to estimate tidal power flux
- Explain why the kinetic-energy-flux method underestimates tidal power
- State measured tidal power flows around Britain
