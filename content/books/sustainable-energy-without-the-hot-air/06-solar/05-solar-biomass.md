## Where to read

Chapter 6, book pages 42–44 (PDF pages 55–57 of the free edition at [withouthotair.com](http://www.withouthotair.com)).

## Learning objectives

- List the four routes to energy from biological systems
- Explain why the "first staging post" gives an upper bound on all bioenergy
- Derive the 24 kWh/d biomass estimate and explain why it is an over-estimate

## Errata that affect this section

- Page 43, Figure 6.11 — the corn-to-ethanol power density "0.02 W/m²" should be "0.048 W/m²" (see also the page 284 erratum).
