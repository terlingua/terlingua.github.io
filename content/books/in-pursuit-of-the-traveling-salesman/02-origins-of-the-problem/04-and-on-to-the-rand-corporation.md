## Learning objectives

- Place RAND at the center of 1950s TSP research.
- Credit Julia Robinson’s naming and LP groundwork.

## Summary

By the end of the 1940s the TSP was a known challenge, and the action had moved from Princeton to the **RAND Corporation** in Santa Monica, following Merrill Flood (p. 38). RAND circulated a list of prize problems that included the TSP, around Fine Hall in 1948–49. The prize was never awarded (p. 38).

**Julia Robinson** took up the TSP in 1949. Her report is the earliest known use of the name. She posed the state-capitals tour from Washington, and her approach laid the groundwork for the RAND breakthrough (pp. 38–39). Nobody knows who coined the name. Flood did not (p. 39).

## In software terms

Public challenges with clear specifications and prizes, from RAND’s list to DIMACS, TSPLIB and the Mona Lisa bounty, draw talent to a problem. Bug bounties and benchmark leaderboards work the same way.
