## Learning objectives

- List the open problems and the book’s outlook.

## Summary

Open questions at publication: Mona Lisa and World challenges, the 4/3rds Conjecture, beating Held–Karp’s n²2ⁿ, and beating Christofides’ 1.5 (p. 211). The TSP is “an addiction” (Papadimitriou, Chvátal). If P ≠ NP, the salesman tests how far focused work can go against intractability (p. 212). *Bash on regardless.*

Chapter 13 of this companion records which of these moved after 2011.
