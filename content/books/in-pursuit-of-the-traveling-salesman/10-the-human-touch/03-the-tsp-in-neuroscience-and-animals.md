## Learning objectives

- Recognize the TSP as an instrument in psychology and animal cognition.

## Summary

- Human differences appear around 20+ cities and correlate modestly with nonverbal intelligence (p. 196).
- **Trail Making** (Halstead–Reitan): connect points in order, timed. The most widely used neuropsych test in a 1990 survey. Fixed layouts, since generating equivalent ones is unsolved (pp. 196–197).
- **Animals:** chimps (Menzel, 1973), vervet monkeys, marmosets, rats, and pigeons on touch screens (Gibson). Incentives improved the pigeons’ tours (pp. 197–198).
