## Learning objectives

- Appreciate the connection between discrete mathematics and art that Cook closes with.

## Summary

Nešetřil (a mathematician and artist) proposes a **Creative Thesis**: deep activities share deep structure (pp. 207–208). Bonn’s Research Institute for Discrete Mathematics houses the Arithmeum, a museum of computing and constructivist art, and leads the world in VLSI optimization, whose chip layouts look like art (pp. 208–210). Cook hopes for more TSP–art connections “as progress is made in understanding the problem’s fundamental complexity” (p. 210).
