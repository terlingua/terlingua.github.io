## Learning objectives

- Apply hazard-versus-risk thinking to chemicals in food
- Know the main contaminants that matter
- Evaluate GMO and organic claims

## How additives are regulated

Regulators (FDA, EFSA, JECFA) set an **acceptable daily intake (ADI)** — usually the highest dose with no
observed adverse effect in animal studies divided by 100. Typical consumer intakes are generally far below
ADIs. Systems are not perfect: in the US, the "self-affirmed GRAS" pathway lets companies declare an ingredient
safe without notifying the FDA, which has been criticised by researchers and policymakers.

## Contaminants that matter

| Contaminant | Main sources | Health concern | Grade |
|------------|--------------|----------------|-------|
| Methylmercury | Large predatory fish | Fetal brain development | **Established** |
| Inorganic arsenic | Rice, some well water | Cancer, development | **Strong** at high exposure |
| Lead | Old pipes, some spices, contaminated products | Child neurodevelopment, no safe level | **Established** |
| Aflatoxins | Mouldy grains and nuts | Liver cancer | **Established** |
| Acrylamide | Fried and browned starches | Animal carcinogen | **Uncertain** for humans at dietary doses |
| Microplastics | Widespread | Unknown | **Uncertain** |
| PFAS | Packaging, water | Some immune, lipid and cancer effects | **Strong** for some PFAS at high exposures; dietary contribution **Uncertain** |

Lead deserves emphasis: the 2023 US recall of cinnamon applesauce pouches after children were poisoned, and
repeated findings of lead in imported spices and some traditional medicines, show that contamination risk is
real for "natural" and traditional products too.

## Biotechnology

| Claim | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| Approved GM foods are as safe to eat as conventional counterparts | **Established** scientific consensus |
| GM foods cause cancer, allergies or infertility | **Not supported** |
| Organic food is more nutritious | **Not supported** in any meaningful way |
| Organic food has lower pesticide residues | **Established** |
| Lower residues produce measurable health benefits | **Uncertain** |
