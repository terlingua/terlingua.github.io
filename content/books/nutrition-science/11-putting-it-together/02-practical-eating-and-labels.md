## Learning objectives

- Translate evidence into daily eating
- Read a nutrition label efficiently

## A practical summary

1. **Eat mostly plants** — vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, nuts and seeds
2. **Choose fats well** — prefer olive, canola and other vegetable oils over butter, lard and tallow
3. **Pick protein sources thoughtfully** — legumes, fish, poultry, dairy and eggs; little processed meat
4. **Drink water** — limit sugary drinks; less alcohol is better, none is best for cancer risk
5. **Watch salt** — most comes from packaged and restaurant food
6. **Cook more, from minimally processed ingredients**, when you can
7. **Keep food safe** — pasteurised dairy, thorough cooking, cold storage
8. **Supplement only for a reason** — folic acid in pre-conception, B12 for vegans, vitamin D for infants and at-risk groups
9. **Ignore miracle claims** — no single food, ritual or product saves or ruins your health
10. **Find a pattern you can sustain** — adherence beats perfection

## Reading a label, in order

1. Serving size and servings per container
2. Calories per serving
3. Added sugars, sodium and saturated fat (lower is better)
4. Fibre (higher is better)
5. The ingredients list (first few ingredients make up most of the product)
