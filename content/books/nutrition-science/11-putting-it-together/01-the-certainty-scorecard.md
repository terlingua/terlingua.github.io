## Learning objectives

- Summarise the book's main conclusions by certainty
- Distinguish genuine scientific uncertainty from manufactured doubt

## Established

- Energy balance governs body-fat change; expenditure falls as weight falls
- Deficiency diseases: vitamin C (scurvy), thiamin (beriberi), niacin (pellagra), vitamin D (rickets), B12, iodine,
  iron, vitamin A
- Folic acid prevents neural tube defects; vitamin K at birth prevents bleeding; vegans need B12
- LDL cholesterol causes atherosclerosis; saturated fat raises LDL relative to unsaturated fat
- Industrial trans fats cause heart disease
- Sodium raises blood pressure; DASH lowers it
- Alcohol causes cancer and other harms with dose
- Processed meat causes colorectal cancer (small absolute risk)
- Free sugars cause tooth decay
- Lifestyle change prevents type 2 diabetes in high-risk adults; major weight loss can induce remission
- Pasteurisation prevents milk-borne disease; raw milk carries much higher infection risk
- High-dose beta-carotene increases lung cancer in smokers
- Early peanut introduction reduces peanut allergy in high-risk infants
- Approved GM foods are as safe as conventional foods

## Strong

- High fibre and whole grain intake lowers chronic-disease risk
- Mediterranean-style patterns reduce cardiovascular events
- Sugary drinks promote weight gain and type 2 diabetes
- Fruit, vegetables, legumes and nuts are associated with lower mortality
- Replacing red and processed meat with plant protein lowers risk
- Higher protein helps older adults and people losing weight retain muscle
- Moderate coffee and tea are safe for most adults
- Ultra-processed foods are associated with poor outcomes and can drive overeating

## Uncertain

- Health effects of eggs at moderate intake
- Effects of full-fat versus low-fat dairy
- Long-term effects of sweeteners and emulsifiers
- Role of the gut microbiome in most diseases; probiotics for healthy people
- Benefits of meal timing and fasting beyond calorie reduction
- Optimal vitamin D level; effect on cancer mortality
- Very-low-dose alcohol effects on total mortality
- Precise mechanisms of ultra-processed food harm
- Whether a defended body-weight "set point" exists
- Long-term safety of lifelong GLP-1 therapy

## Not supported

- Detoxes, cleanses, alkaline diets, blood-type diets
- Raw milk health claims
- Cow urine, dung and other sacred-animal medical remedies
- Diet cures for cancer or type 1 diabetes
- Antioxidant supplements for disease prevention
- Seed-oil toxicity at normal intakes
- MSG and sugar-hyperactivity scares
- Homeopathy, colloidal silver, IgG food-intolerance tests
