## Learning objectives

- Distinguish type 1 and type 2 diabetes
- Know the prevention and remission trials
- Recognise unsupported "cures"

## Prevention and remission

| Claim | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| Lifestyle intervention prevents or delays type 2 diabetes in high-risk adults | **Established** |
| Substantial weight loss can put early type 2 diabetes into remission | **Established** (DiRECT, bariatric surgery) |
| GLP-1 drugs and tirzepatide lower glucose and weight markedly | **Established** |
| Low-carbohydrate diets lower HbA1c in the short term | **Strong** |
| Any specific spice or supplement cures diabetes | **Not supported** |
| Diet can replace insulin in type 1 diabetes | **Not supported** — and dangerous |
