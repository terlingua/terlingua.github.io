## Learning objectives

- Rank dietary factors in cancer by strength of evidence
- Reject cancer-cure diets
- Understand why single-food claims mislead

## Estimated scale

In the UK, Cancer Research UK estimates about 4 in 10 cancer cases are preventable, with smoking first and
excess weight second. Alcohol, low fibre and processed meat each contribute a few percent.

## Evidence summary (WCRF/AICR and IARC)

| Exposure | Cancer | Grade |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Alcohol | Mouth, throat, oesophagus, liver, breast, colorectum | **Established** |
| Excess body fat | 13+ sites | **Established** |
| Processed meat | Colorectal | **Established** (Group 1) |
| Red meat | Colorectal | **Strong** (probable) |
| Aflatoxins | Liver | **Established** |
| Salt-preserved foods | Stomach | **Strong** |
| Whole grains, fibre | Colorectal (protective) | **Strong** (probable) |
| Dairy and calcium | Colorectal (protective) | **Strong** (probable) |
| Physical activity | Colon, breast, endometrium (protective) | **Established** to **Strong** |
| Very hot drinks above 65 °C | Oesophagus | **Strong** (IARC 2A) |
| Sugar "feeds" cancer | — | **Not supported** as a direct mechanism |
| Alkaline diet, juice fasts, apricot kernels (laetrile), coffee enemas | Cure | **Not supported**; laetrile can cause cyanide poisoning |
