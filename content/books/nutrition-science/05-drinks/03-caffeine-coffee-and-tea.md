## Learning objectives

- Know safe caffeine intakes
- Interpret coffee research appropriately

## Caffeine content (approximate)

| Drink | Caffeine |
|-------|----------|
| Brewed coffee, 240 ml | 80–120 mg |
| Espresso, single shot | about 60–75 mg |
| Black tea, 240 ml | 40–60 mg |
| Green tea, 240 ml | 25–45 mg |
| Cola, 355 ml | 30–40 mg |
| Energy drink, 250 ml | about 80 mg (larger cans 150–300 mg) |

## Evidence summary

| Claim | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| Up to about 400 mg/day is safe for most healthy adults | **Strong** |
| Caffeine improves alertness and endurance performance | **Established** |
| Coffee causes heart disease in the general population | **Not supported** |
| Moderate coffee lowers type 2 diabetes risk | **Strong** association, causality **Uncertain** |
| Coffee "stunts growth" | **Not supported** |
| High caffeine in pregnancy is linked to lower birth weight | **Strong** association; hence the 200 mg limit |
| Caffeine withdrawal is real (headache, fatigue) | **Established** |
