## Learning objectives

- Know the critical nutrients and hazards in pregnancy
- Understand infant feeding evidence
- Recognise how early-allergen guidance changed

## Pregnancy essentials

| Nutrient or practice | Recommendation | Grade |
|----------------------|----------------|-------|
| Folic acid | 400 µg/day from before conception through week 12 (higher doses for some) | **Established** |
| Iodine | 220 µg/day; many guidelines suggest a 150 µg supplement | **Strong** |
| Iron | 27 mg/day RDA; supplement if deficient | **Established** |
| Vitamin D | 10 µg (400 IU) daily in UK guidance | **Strong** |
| Alcohol | None | **Established** (harm), precautionary (low dose) |
| Caffeine | Below 200 mg/day | **Strong** |
| Low-mercury fish | 2–3 servings/week | **Strong** |
| Listeria-risk foods | Avoid unpasteurised dairy, deli meats unless reheated, refrigerated smoked fish | **Established** |

## Infancy

- Vitamin K injection at birth — **Established**
- Vitamin D 400 IU/day for breastfed infants — **Established**
- Solid foods around six months, not before four — **Strong**
- Early introduction of peanut and egg — **Established** for peanut in high-risk infants (LEAP)
- No honey before 12 months — **Established**
- No added salt or sugar — **Strong**
