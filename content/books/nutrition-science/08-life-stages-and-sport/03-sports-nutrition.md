## Learning objectives

- Set protein and carbohydrate targets for athletes
- Identify the few supplements with good evidence
- Recognise low energy availability

## Targets

| Need | Guidance | Grade |
|------|---------|-------|
| Protein | 1.2–2.0 g/kg/day, about 0.3 g/kg per meal | **Strong** |
| Carbohydrate (daily) | 3–5 g/kg light training; 6–10 g/kg heavy; 8–12 g/kg very heavy endurance | **Strong** |
| Carbohydrate during long events | 30–90 g/hour depending on duration | **Strong** |
| Fluid | Drink to thirst; avoid weight gain during events | **Strong** |

## Supplements with evidence

| Supplement | Benefit | Grade |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Caffeine 3–6 mg/kg | Endurance and some high-intensity performance | **Established** |
| Creatine | Strength, power, repeated sprints | **Established** |
| Nitrate | Endurance in non-elite athletes | **Strong** |
| Beta-alanine | Efforts of 1–10 minutes | **Strong** |
| Sodium bicarbonate | High-intensity efforts; gut side effects | **Strong** |
| BCAAs, glutamine, testosterone "boosters" | None meaningful | **Not supported** |
