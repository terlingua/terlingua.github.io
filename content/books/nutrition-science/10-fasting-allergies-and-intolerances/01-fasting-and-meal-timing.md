## Learning objectives

- Evaluate intermittent fasting against calorie restriction
- Judge breakfast and meal-timing claims

## Types of fasting

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Time-restricted eating (TRE) | Eating within a 6–10 hour window daily |
| 5:2 | Two non-consecutive low-calorie days (about 500–600 kcal) each week |
| Alternate-day fasting | Fasting or very low intake every other day |
| Prolonged fasting | More than 48 hours; requires medical supervision |

## Evidence

| Claim | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| Intermittent fasting produces weight loss | **Established** — through reduced intake |
| It beats equal-calorie daily restriction | **Not supported** |
| It improves insulin sensitivity beyond weight loss | **Uncertain** (some early TRE studies suggest yes) |
| Fasting extends human lifespan | **Uncertain** — animal data only |
| Breakfast is needed for weight control | **Not supported** |
| Eating in line with circadian rhythm (earlier in the day) is metabolically favourable | **Uncertain** to **Strong** for glucose; outcome data lacking |
