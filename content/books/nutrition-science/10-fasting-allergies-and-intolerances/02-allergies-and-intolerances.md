## Learning objectives

- Distinguish allergy, autoimmune disease and intolerance
- Know validated diagnostic approaches
- Recognise invalid tests

## Comparison

| Condition | Mechanism | Diagnosis | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| IgE food allergy | Immune (IgE) | History plus skin-prick or specific IgE; oral food challenge | Avoidance, adrenaline auto-injector; oral immunotherapy for some |
| Coeliac disease | Autoimmune | tTG-IgA blood test and intestinal biopsy, on a gluten-containing diet | Lifelong strict gluten-free diet |
| Lactose intolerance | Low lactase enzyme | Clinical history; hydrogen breath test | Limit lactose; lactase enzymes; lactose-free products |
| IBS (FODMAP-sensitive) | Gut sensitivity, motility | Clinical criteria (Rome IV) | Low-FODMAP diet with reintroduction, among others |
| Non-coeliac gluten/wheat sensitivity | Unclear | Exclusion of coeliac and wheat allergy | Individual trial of reduction |

## Invalid tests

Applied kinesiology, hair analysis, IgG food panels, electrodermal ("Vega") testing and pulse tests have no
diagnostic validity for food allergy or intolerance. Grade - **Not supported**.
