## Learning objectives

- Compare popular diets on the evidence
- Understand why maintenance is the hard part
- Know what GLP-1 drugs and surgery can and cannot do

## Diets compared

| Claim | Grade | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Any diet that creates a sustained energy deficit produces weight loss | **Established** | Metabolic-ward and long-term trials |
| Low-carb beats low-fat for long-term weight loss | **Not supported** | DIETFITS and meta-analyses: small or no difference beyond 12 months |
| Low-carb diets can help people with type 2 diabetes lower blood glucose | **Strong** | Short-term glycaemic benefits; long-term data weaker |
| Higher-protein diets help preserve muscle and increase satiety during weight loss | **Strong** | Modest effects |
| Ultra-processed diets promote overeating | **Strong but limited** | One small ward RCT plus cohorts (see the processed-food section) |
| Blood type, metabolic type or "genetic" diets beat generic advice | **Not supported** | No trial benefit |
| Meal replacements improve short-term weight loss | **Strong** | Used successfully in DiRECT and Look AHEAD |

## The individual variation problem

In DIETFITS, average loss was about 5–6 kg in both arms, but individuals ranged from losing more than 25 kg
to gaining 10 kg. Averages hide the fact that the best diet for an individual is the one they can keep.

## Drugs

GLP-1 receptor agonists and related drugs changed obesity treatment:

| Drug | Trial | Mean weight loss vs placebo |
|------|-------|----------------------------|
| Semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly injection | STEP 1 (2021) | about 14.9% vs 2.4% at 68 weeks |
| Tirzepatide 15 mg weekly injection | SURMOUNT-1 (2022) | about 20.9% vs 3.1% at 72 weeks |

Oral options followed: an oral semaglutide product for weight management was approved in the US in
late 2025, and orforglipron (Foundayo), a small-molecule oral GLP-1 agonist, in April 2026.

- **Established:** large average weight loss while taking them
- **Established:** semaglutide reduced major cardiovascular events by about 20% in SELECT
- **Established:** most weight is regained after stopping (STEP 1 extension)
- **Uncertain:** long-term (decades) safety; best strategies for preserving muscle; effects of lifelong use

Common side effects are nausea, vomiting, constipation and diarrhoea. Adequate protein and resistance
training are sensible during drug-induced weight loss, because some of the weight lost is lean tissue.

## Surgery

The Swedish Obese Subjects study and others show sustained weight loss, remission of type 2 diabetes in many
patients, and lower mortality. Lifelong supplementation (B12, iron, calcium, vitamin D) is required after
most procedures.
