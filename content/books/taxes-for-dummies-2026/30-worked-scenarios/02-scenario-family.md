## Learning objectives

- Decide whether itemizing helps when deductions barely exceed the standard deduction
- Apply the child tax credit and see marginal benefits
- Compare a dependent care FSA with the credit

## The facts

Sam and Alex file jointly for 2025 with $150,000 of combined W-2 wages and two children aged 4 and 9. They paid $14,000 of mortgage interest, $8,000 of property tax, $7,000 of state income tax and $3,000 to charity. Daycare and after-school care cost $12,000; they have no dependent care FSA this year but could elect $5,000 next year.

## How the pieces fit

Itemize only if the total beats $31,500 (Chapters 4, 11). The CTC is $2,200 per child under 17, phasing out above $400,000 (Chapter 16). The dependent care credit for AGI over $43,000 is 20% of up to $6,000 (two children). An FSA excludes up to $5,000 from income and FICA, reducing the expenses available for the credit.
