## Learning objectives

- Compute SE tax, its deduction, QBI and income tax together
- See how a SEP contribution interacts with QBI
- Set safe-harbor estimates for the next year

## The facts

Jordan is single, a freelance developer, with $90,000 of 2025 Schedule C net profit and no other income. Jordan takes the standard deduction and is considering a SEP-IRA contribution.

## How the pieces fit

SE tax = 15.3% × 92.35% × net profit (Chapter 17). Half of SE tax is an adjustment. QBI = net profit minus half SE tax (and minus SEP and SE health deductions), and the QBI deduction = 20% × the lesser of QBI or taxable income before QBI (Chapter 23). A SEP contribution is limited to 20% of (net profit − half SE tax) (Chapter 22). Safe-harbor estimates are based on total tax including SE tax (Chapter 17).
