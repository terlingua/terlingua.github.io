## Learning objectives

- Recall the corrections most likely to change a real return or decision
- Know where the book's guidance is out of date

## The ideas

Errors and outdated statements flagged throughout this companion, ranked roughly by how much money they can cost:

1. **Failure-to-file penalty** is 5% a month (max 25%), not 0.5% (p. 435): always file on time.
2. **Home equity interest** isn't deductible unless the loan improves the home (pp. 538, 555, 583).
3. **Converting a rental to a residence** doesn't shelter rental-period gain (nonqualified use) (p. 530).
4. **Inherited crypto** gets a stepped-up basis like other property (p. 336).
5. **Estate portability** lets a surviving spouse use the unused exemption — the book omits it (p. 565).
6. **QCDs** start at 70½, not 73 (p. 488); the **still-working RMD exception** covers only the current employer's plan.
7. **Dependent care credit** doesn't phase out at $125,000–$438,000 (that was 2021) (p. 547); **CTC** phases out to zero, not to $2,000 (p. 362).
8. **Adoption credit** is partly refundable ($5,000) from 2025 (p. 365).
9. **Simplified home office** is $5/sq ft, max $1,500 (pp. 292, 383).
10. **Backup withholding** is 24% (p. 399); **bank levies** are held 21 days (p. 401); the **90-day letter** runs from mailing (p. 414).
11. **QBI** is limited by taxable income and computed on QBI net of SE-tax and other deductions (p. 500).
12. **Wash sales** include purchases 30 days *before* a sale (p. 524).
13. **Estimated tax**: the 110% test uses prior-year AGI; 2026 dates are June 15 and September 15 (pp. 372–373).
14. **Capital gain brackets** in Table 24-1 mix years; use taxable income, not AGI (pp. 512–513).
15. **Kids on payroll**: FICA exemption is under 18, sole proprietorships and parent partnerships only (p. 504).
