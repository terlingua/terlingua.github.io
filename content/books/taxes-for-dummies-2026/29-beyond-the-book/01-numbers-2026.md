## Learning objectives

- Know the core 2026 figures and apply the brackets
- Distinguish 2025 (book) from 2026 (next return) amounts

## The ideas

From Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and related IRS releases, for income earned in **2026** (returns filed in 2027):

- **Standard deduction**: $16,100 single/MFS, $32,200 joint, $24,150 head of household (plus additional amounts for 65+/blind, and the separate $6,000 senior deduction through 2028).
- **Brackets** (single): 10% to $12,400; 12% to $50,400; 22% to $105,700; 24% to $201,775; 32% to $256,225; 35% to $640,600; 37% above. Joint: $24,800 / $100,800 / $211,400 / $403,550 / $512,450 / $768,700.
- **Long-term gains and qualified dividends**: 0% to $49,450 single, $98,900 joint, $66,200 HOH; 15% to $545,500 / $613,700 / $579,600; 20% above.
- **AMT exemption**: $90,100 single, $140,200 joint, phasing out at 50 cents per dollar from $500,000 / $1,000,000 (OBBBA lowered the thresholds and doubled the phase-out rate).
- **Child tax credit** $2,200 (refundable up to $1,700); **EITC** maximum $8,231 (three or more children).
- **Retirement**: 401(k)/403(b)/457 $24,500; catch-up $8,000 (ages 60–63: $11,250); IRA $7,500 (+$1,100 catch-up at 50+, now indexed); Roth catch-ups required for prior-year FICA wages over $150,000.
- **HSA**: $4,400 self-only, $8,750 family. **Social Security wage base**: $184,500. **Standard mileage**: 72.5 cents business.
- **Gift tax annual exclusion** $19,000; **estate exemption** $15 million.
- **SALT cap** $40,400 (phase-down above $505,000 MAGI); **excess business loss** limit $256,000 ($512,000 joint); **foreign earned income exclusion** $132,900.
