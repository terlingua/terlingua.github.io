## Learning objectives

- Compute the 2025 child tax credit, refundable portion and phase-out (with a correction)
- Apply the SSN requirements added by OBBBA
- Qualify for the saver's credit

## The ideas

**Child tax credit** (Form 8812, pp. 361–363): **$2,200** per qualifying child under 17 at year end for 2025 (indexed; $2,200 for 2026, $2,300 for 2027). Up to **$1,700** per child is refundable as the additional child tax credit (15% of earned income over $2,500). **Credit for other dependents**: $500, nonrefundable (older children, parents, college students). Phase-out: $50 per $1,000 (or part) of MAGI over $200,000 ($400,000 joint). *Correction:* the book says the phase-out stops at $2,000 of credit; it doesn't — it continues to zero. (The $2,000 floor was a 2021 American Rescue Plan rule.) OBBBA requires the child's SSN and an SSN for the taxpayer (for joint filers, at least one spouse).

A noncustodial parent with a signed Form 8332 can claim the CTC; Form 8901 handles qualifying children who aren't dependents (p. 362).

**Saver's credit** (Form 8880, p. 363): 50%, 20% or 10% of up to $2,000 of retirement contributions per person (max $1,000; $2,000 per couple), for AGI up to $79,000 joint, $59,250 HOH, $39,500 others (2025). You must be 18+ (born before January 1, 2008), not a dependent, and not a full-time student in any five months. Distributions in the lookback period reduce it. From 2027 it becomes the "Saver's Match" deposited into a retirement account (SECURE 2.0).
