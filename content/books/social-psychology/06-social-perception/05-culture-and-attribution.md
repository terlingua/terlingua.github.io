## Learning objectives

- Explain how holistic and analytic thinking affect attributions
- Describe cultural differences in the fundamental attribution error
- Describe cultural differences in other attributional biases, such as self-serving attributions

## Key ideas

Holistic thinkers attend to context, analytic thinkers to the person (p. 160). Morris and Peng (1994) found U.S. newspapers explained mass murders more dispositionally and Chinese newspapers more situationally. People in collectivist cultures still make the FAE, especially under time pressure, but are more likely to correct for the situation when it is salient (p. 161). Self-serving biases also appear less strongly in some East Asian samples, where self-criticism and modesty are valued (p. 163).
