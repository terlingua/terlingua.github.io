## Learning objectives

- Explain how sustainable behavior and nature relate to happiness
- Identify the four controllable predictors of happiness
- Explain the relationship between money, materialism and happiness
- Explain why people often get the recipe for happiness backward

## Key ideas

Doing good for the planet and being in nature make people happier (p. 417). Four controllable influences on happiness (pp. 418–420): **satisfying relationships**, **a sense of purpose**, **experiences rather than things**, and **helping others**. Materialistic people are less happy; money's relationship with happiness is debated, but spending on experiences and on others beats spending on possessions.

**Do people know?** (p. 421): many get it backward, focusing on wealth and possessions and too little on relationships, purpose and helping. Americans are increasingly isolated. The "Life A versus Life B" comparison shows a sustainable life can also be a happier one.
